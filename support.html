YorubaGo Support

YorubaGo is a Yoruba-learning app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It has structured lessons, spaced-repetition review, tone practice, an arcade of short games, trophies, native-speaker audio, and a record-and-compare speaking exercise. Everything works offline.

Which units are free?

Units 1 and 2 of Level 1 are free. The one-time Level 1 Full Course purchase unlocks the rest of Level 1 (Units 3 to 8). Future levels will be sold separately.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. You can also tap it on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad, and Mac?

Sign into the same iCloud account on each device and make sure iCloud is turned on for YorubaGo in your device settings. Progress syncs through your own iCloud, and it can take a minute or two to appear. The app also works fully without iCloud, with progress kept on that device.

The microphone isn't working

Go to Settings, then Privacy, then Microphone on iPhone or iPad (System Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone on Mac) and allow YorubaGo. You can skip recording and still use listen-and-repeat.

I can't hear any audio

Check that the silent switch is off, the volume is up, and no Bluetooth device is capturing the sound. Then close and reopen the app.
