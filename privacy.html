YorubaGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026. Developer: Adedoyin Adeyemo.

YorubaGo does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server run by the developer. This page explains what that means in practice.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress (completed lessons, review schedule, points, trophies, and settings) is stored on your device. The developer never receives it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, YorubaGo uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage so your progress can follow you between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. That data is held by Apple in your own iCloud account, under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access it. If you turn off iCloud for the app or sign out, the app keeps working with progress stored on the device only.

Microphone

The speaking exercise asks for microphone access so you can record yourself and compare it with the native speaker. Recordings are kept in memory only for playback during the exercise. They are not saved to storage, not uploaded, and not shared. You can decline permission and still use the rest of the app. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

Purchases

YorubaGo is free to download. Units 1 and 2 are free, and a one-time in-app purchase unlocks the Full Course. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details or personal information.

What we do not use

  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No user accounts
  • No network requests made by the app for its content; all lessons and audio are included in the app

Children

YorubaGo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads and no social features. The app has no network connection of its own; the only time it leaves the app is when you tap the Privacy Policy or Support links in Settings, which open those web pages in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
